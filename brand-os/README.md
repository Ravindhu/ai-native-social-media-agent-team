# Brand OS

The playbook every department reads before it writes. Fill these files for your brand once; the agents read them on every run.

| Folder | What lives here | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| `brand-brain/` | Positioning, messaging bank, tone rules, voice guide | every department |
| `content/` | Brief templates, content calendar, hook bank, SEO checklist | Content Engine |
| `conversation/` | Auto-reply scripts, FAQ library, routing rules | Leads & Conversations |
| `creative/` | Asset library, video scripts, visual specs | Creative Studio |
| `reporting/` | Attribution, KPI model, margin rules | Results & ROI |
| `reputation/` | GBP response, mentions rules, review flow | Reviews & Reputation |
| `signals-seo/` | Keyword map, ranking targets, SERP rules, tracking setup | Search Visibility, Market Radar |
| `social/` | Channel playbooks, hashtag rules, posting schedule | Publishing & Social |

Files marked _Template_ are starting points. Replace the example text with your brand's own.
