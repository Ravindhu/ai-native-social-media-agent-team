# GBP response (Template)

## Five stars
"Thank you, (name). We're glad (specific detail) worked for you. See you again soon."

## Three or four stars
"Thanks for the feedback, (name). We're sorry (specific issue) fell short. We're looking at it."

## One or two stars
Draft only. Goes to `queue/needs-your-ok.md`. Never offer a refund, a date or a discount in a reply.
