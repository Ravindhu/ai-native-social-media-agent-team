# Channel playbooks (Template)

## Instagram
- Reels: 3–5 per week. Carousels: 1–2 per week. Stories: daily.

## TikTok
- 3–4 vertical videos per week, native style, captions on.

## LinkedIn
- 2–3 posts per week. Lead with a specific point of view, not a pitch.

## Facebook
- 3–4 posts per week. Reshare Instagram Reels with a native caption.

## Google Business Profile
- 1 update per week. Keep photos and hours current.
