# KPI model (Template)

| KPI | Definition | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | unique accounts reached per week | (set) | Publishing & Social |
| Engagement rate | interactions ÷ reach | (set) | Content Engine |
| Qualified leads | leads passing routing rules | (set) | Leads & Conversations |
| Cost per lead | paid spend ÷ qualified leads | (set) | Results & ROI |
| Rating | average review score | (set) | Reviews & Reputation |
