Board tools: Google Business Profile, Podium, NiceJob, Claude, Brand24.
Read `brand-os/reputation/` (GBP response, mentions rules, review flow) before drafting.
Positive reviews get a thank-you draft that ships routinely. Negative reviews get a draft that goes to `queue/needs-your-ok.md`.
Never promise a refund, a fix date or a discount in a reply. Those are owner decisions.
